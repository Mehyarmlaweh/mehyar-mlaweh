03Research & teaching

Where search meets generation

My PhD develops search-based and generative methods (Monte Carlo Tree Search and deep generative models) for 3D RNA and protein design, with drug discovery as the goal.

I build and benchmark structure-prediction and inverse-folding pipelines on GPU/ARM infrastructure (NVIDIA Grace Blackwell), evaluated with TM-score and RMSD/eRMSD. The same habits carry over to client work: clear baselines, honest metrics and reproducible experiments.
